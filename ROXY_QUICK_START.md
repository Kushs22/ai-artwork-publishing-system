# ArtFlow — Quick start for Roxy

**Your link (bookmark this):** [artflow-bark-grain.streamlit.app](https://artflow-bark-grain.streamlit.app)

No install. Works on Mac, iPad, or phone browser.

---

## Every new artwork (5 steps)

1. Open the link above  
2. Go to **Batch Studio**  
3. Upload your JPG/PNG (one or many)  
4. Tick **Instagram** and **Pinterest** (and others if needed)  
5. Click **AI Auto Prep all**

Wait until processing finishes.

---

## Get your files

- Expand **Batch results** or open **Review** for one piece  
- Click **Download ZIP (all files)** — images + caption text  
- Or use **Save folder to disk** if Kush set up your Mac with a local copy  

Copy captions into Instagram / Pinterest. Upload the sized images from the ZIP.

---

## Important

- **You approve everything** — edit captions so they sound like you  
- **Nothing posts automatically**  
- **Your original photo is never changed** — only new files are created  
- AI uses Google Gemini — do not upload private client photos without consent
